01 Reproduction

Paper uses 4 GPUs, effective batch 32k tokens. We have 1 GPU, so 8k batch. Setting UPDATE_FREQ=4 matches it.

1-GPU 32k batch (UPDATE_FREQ=4): 0.04 BLEU. Gap was entirely the batch size.

02 Degradation by word order

All three on 130k pairs, 32k effective batch. chrF++ drop from peak to lowest latency.

Same ranking with 8k batch (9.0%, 4.8%, 2.9%) — just flatter curves at 32k.

Predicted order: SOV > VSO > SVO. Actual: SOV > SVO > VSO. Korean worst, as expected. But Vietnamese (SVO, same as English) loses more than MSA (VSO). Word order alone does not explain it.

Absolute scores differ 2.5x across languages (Korean 20, MSA 39, Vietnamese 49). In raw points Vietnamese drops most (1.38), not Korean (0.91). Percentage vs points give opposite rankings.
